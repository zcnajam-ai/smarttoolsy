Platform limits

    The character counter shows how many characters are in your text, with and without spaces, as you type. It also counts words, lines and bytes, works out how many SMS messages the text would take, and compares it against the limits of popular platforms so you can see at a glance whether your post, caption or title will fit.

    How characters are counted

    • Characters includes letters, numbers, punctuation, spaces and line breaks. Each emoji counts as one character here, though some platforms count certain emoji as two.
    • Without spaces leaves out spaces, tabs and line breaks, a figure some assignments and forms ask for.
    • Bytes is the size in UTF-8, useful for database fields and technical limits. Accented letters take two bytes and most emoji four.

    Frequently asked questions

    Do spaces count as characters?

    Yes, on almost every platform and form. That’s why the main total includes spaces; the “without spaces” figure is shown separately.

    Why does my text message split into several messages?

    A standard SMS holds 160 characters. Longer texts are split into parts of 153 characters each. Any emoji or special character switches the whole message to a different encoding with only 70 characters per message (67 per part).

    Is my text saved?

    No. Counting happens in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.
