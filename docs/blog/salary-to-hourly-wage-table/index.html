Published 2026-10-27

A salary of $60,000 a year works out to about $28.85 an hour, based on a standard 40-hour week for 52 weeks (2,080 hours). The table below shows the same conversion for other common salaries, and the salary to hourly calculator handles any amount and schedule.

How to convert any salary

Hourly = yearly salary ÷ (hours per week × weeks per year)

For the standard 2,080 hours, a quick estimate is to halve the salary and drop the last three zeros: $60,000 → $30 an hour (the exact figure is $28.85). Going the other way, double the hourly rate and add three zeros: $25 an hour → about $50,000 a year (exactly $52,000).

Different schedules

If you work 37.5 hours a week, $60,000 is $30.77 an hour. If an hourly job comes with two unpaid weeks off, use 50 weeks: $28.85 × 40 × 50 = $57,700 a year. Enter your own hours and weeks in the calculator.

Why your paycheck is smaller

All of these figures are gross pay. Take-home pay is reduced by federal income tax, Social Security (6.2%) and Medicare (1.45%), any state and local income tax, and deductions such as health insurance and retirement contributions. Depending on your state and choices, take-home pay is often somewhere around 70–80% of gross for a single filer at these incomes, but it varies widely, so use a paycheck calculator for your state for a precise figure.

Comparing a salaried and an hourly job

  • Overtime: hourly, non-exempt workers earn 1.5× for hours over 40 a week; many salaried roles don’t.
  • Benefits: health insurance, retirement matching and paid time off can be worth thousands of dollars a year.
  • Actual hours: a $70,000 salary that regularly requires 50-hour weeks works out to about $26.92 an hour.
