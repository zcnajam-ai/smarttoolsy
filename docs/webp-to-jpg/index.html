WebP to JPG / PNG Converter

Convert WebP (and other images) to JPG or PNG in a batch.

    WebP is a modern image format that many websites use to load faster, but plenty of apps, printers and upload forms still don’t accept it. This converter turns WebP images into JPG or PNG files that work everywhere. Convert one image or a batch, then download them separately or as a ZIP. It also converts JPG and other images to PNG. Everything runs in your browser.

    How to convert WebP to JPG

    1. Choose one or more WebP files.
    2. Pick JPG (smaller files) or PNG (keeps transparency).
    3. Download the converted images, or all of them as a ZIP.

    JPG or PNG?

    • JPG for photos: small files, accepted by every app and website. Transparent areas are filled with the background color you choose.
    • PNG for graphics, logos and anything with transparency: lossless, but larger for photos.

    Frequently asked questions

    Why do images download as WebP?

    Websites serve WebP because it’s smaller than JPG and PNG at the same quality, so pages load faster. When you save an image from such a site, you get the WebP file.

    Does converting reduce quality?

    Converting to PNG keeps every pixel. Converting to JPG uses lossy compression, but at quality 90+ the difference is hard to see.

    Are my files uploaded?

    No. Conversion uses your browser’s built-in image tools, so files never leave your device.
