Background Remover

AI cut-outs with a transparent, white or colored background.

Download PNG

The background remover cuts the main subject out of a photo automatically. Choose an image and an AI model finds the person, product, pet or object in front, removes everything behind it, and gives you a PNG with a transparent background. You can also put the subject on white, black or any color you like, ready for a product listing, profile picture, presentation or design.

Unlike most background removers, this one doesn’t upload your photo. The AI runs inside your browser, so the image stays on your device. It’s free, with no sign-up, no watermark and no credits to buy.

How to remove a background

  1. Click “Choose a photo”, drag one in, or paste an image with Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on a Mac).
  2. Wait a few seconds. The first time, your browser downloads the AI model (about 5 MB); after that it’s instant.
  3. Pick Transparent, White, Black or a custom color.
  4. Click Download. Transparent images save as PNG; images with a color background save as JPG.

What works best

  • One clear subject that stands out from the background: a product on a table, a person, a pet, a car.
  • Good contrast and lighting. A dark subject on a dark background is harder to separate.
  • Sharp photos. Blurry edges make blurry cut-outs.

Fine details such as flyaway hair, fur, glass and smoke are the hardest part for any background remover. The tool keeps soft edges where it isn’t sure, which usually looks more natural than a hard cut. For a perfect result on a tricky image, use it as a starting point and touch up the edges in an editor.

Common uses

  • Online store photos: marketplaces such as Amazon ask for product images on a pure white background.
  • Profile pictures and headshots on a clean, consistent color.
  • Stickers, logos and designs that need a transparent PNG.
  • Slides and documents where a cut-out looks cleaner than a rectangle.

To get the size right afterwards, use the image resizer or image cropper. Before sharing photos online, the metadata remover strips hidden location data.

Frequently asked questions

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. The AI model is downloaded to your browser and the image is processed on your device. SmartToolsy never receives your photo.

Is the background remover really free?

Yes. There are no credits, watermarks or limits on resolution beyond 4,096 pixels on the longest side, which covers almost every use.

Why is it slow the first time?

Your browser downloads the AI model once (about 5 MB) and the engine that runs it. After that it’s cached, and each photo takes a second or two depending on your device.

Which AI model does it use?

U-2-Net, an open-source salient-object detection model released under the Apache 2.0 license, run with Microsoft’s ONNX Runtime for the web.

Can I remove the background from a logo?

Usually, yes, if the logo stands out clearly. For simple flat logos on a solid color, an editor’s “magic wand” or “remove color” option can give even crisper edges.
