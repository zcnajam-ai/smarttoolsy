About SmartToolsy

SmartToolsy is a collection of free online tools for the small jobs that come up every day: counting the words in an essay, converting a recipe from cups to millilitres, checking a loan payment, making a QR code for a shop window or shrinking a photo before emailing it.

Why we built it

Many tool websites are slow, cluttered or ask you to upload files and create an account for a ten-second task. We wanted the opposite: pages that load fast, do one job well and respect your privacy. That is why every tool on SmartToolsy runs in your browser. Your text, images and numbers are processed on your own device and are not sent to us.

How we keep tools accurate

Each calculator uses standard, published formulas, such as the World Health Organization BMI categories, the standard amortization formula for loans and exact international unit definitions. Every tool page explains its method so you can check the result yourself. We test tools against worked examples before publishing them and fix any issue reported to us.

Who runs SmartToolsy

SmartToolsy is an independent website run by SmartToolsy, a small team of web developers and designers. We build and maintain the tools and write the guides ourselves.
