Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-30

This Privacy Policy explains what information SmartToolsy (“we”, “us”) collects when you visit https://www.smarttoolsy.tech (the “Site”), how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information you enter into tools

Our tools run in your web browser. Text, numbers, files and images you enter into a tool are processed on your device and are not sent to or stored on our servers. When you close or reload the page, that input is gone. A few preferences, such as light or dark theme, may be saved in your browser’s local storage so the Site remembers them; you can clear this at any time through your browser settings.

Tools that read a live web page work differently because they have to visit another website. The SEO Checker, and the “load from page” buttons in the SERP Preview Tool and Keyword Density Checker, send the web address you enter to our server, which downloads that public page, its robots.txt and sitemap, and returns the results; we don’t save the reports, although our hosting provider may keep standard request logs for a short time. The Page Speed Test sends the address from your browser to Google PageSpeed Insights, and Google’s privacy policy applies to that request. Only enter addresses of public pages.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information when you request a page, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the page requested and the date and time. This is used to deliver the Site, keep it secure and understand overall traffic. We may use privacy-friendly analytics or Google Analytics to see which pages are popular; this data is aggregated and we do not use it to identify you.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show advertisements, which helps keep the tools free. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • To learn more about how Google uses data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Visitors from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent message so they can choose whether to allow personalized ads and cookies. You can change your choice at any time using the privacy settings link shown by that message.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data held about you, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. Because our tools do not collect what you type, we usually hold very little information about individual visitors. Residents of California have rights under the CCPA, including the right to know what personal information is collected and to opt out of its “sale” or “sharing” for targeted advertising, which you can do through the ad settings links above. To make a request, email contact@smarttoolsy.tech.

Children

The Site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Links to other sites

Our pages may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to contact@smarttoolsy.tech.
