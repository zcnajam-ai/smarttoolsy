Image Cropper

Crop to 1:1, 16:9, 4:5, 9:16 or a circle.

The image cropper trims a photo to exactly the part you want. Drag the frame to choose the area, or lock it to a common shape: square for profile pictures, 4:5 for Instagram portraits, 16:9 for YouTube and presentations, or 9:16 for stories and reels. Tick “Circle crop” for round profile images with transparent corners. Your image is cropped on your own device.

How to crop an image

  1. Choose an image.
  2. Pick an aspect ratio, or leave it on Free.
  3. Drag the frame to move it and drag its corners to resize. For exact numbers, type the X, Y, width and height in pixels.
  4. Click “Crop and download”.

Common aspect ratios

RatioUsed for
1:1Profile pictures, Instagram square posts, product photos
4:5Instagram portrait posts
16:9YouTube thumbnails, slides, laptop and TV screens
9:16Instagram and Facebook Stories, Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts
3:2Standard camera photos and 4 × 6 inch prints

After cropping, use the image resizer to hit an exact pixel size, or the background remover to cut out the subject.

Frequently asked questions

Does cropping reduce quality?

No. Cropping keeps the original pixels in the area you choose; it only removes the rest. JPG files are saved again at high quality.

How do I crop a picture into a circle?

Choose 1:1 so the frame is square, position it, tick “Circle crop” and download. The result is a PNG with transparent corners.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Cropping happens in your browser and nothing is sent to a server.
