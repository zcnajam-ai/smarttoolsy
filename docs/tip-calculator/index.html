The tip calculator works out how much to tip and what everyone owes when you split the bill. Enter the bill, choose a tip percentage, and set the number of people. You can tip on the amount before tax and round each share up to a whole dollar to make paying easier.

How to calculate a tip

Tip = bill × tip percentage ÷ 100
Each person = (bill + tip) ÷ number of people

A quick mental shortcut for 20%: move the decimal point one place left to get 10%, then double it. On a $64.50 bill, 10% is $6.45, so 20% is $12.90.

How much to tip in the US

Customs vary by region and service, but these are common ranges:

ServiceTypical tip
Sit-down restaurant15–20%, more for great service
Bar$1–2 per drink or 15–20% of the tab
Food delivery15–20%, with a few dollars minimum
Taxi or rideshare15–20%
Hair salon or barber15–20%

Frequently asked questions

Should I tip on the total before or after tax?

Either is acceptable. Etiquette guides traditionally suggest tipping on the pre-tax amount, but many people tip on the total for simplicity. Tick “Tip on the amount before tax” and enter the tax to calculate it the traditional way.

Is a service charge the same as a tip?

Not always. Check the bill: if a service charge or gratuity is already included, an additional tip is optional.
