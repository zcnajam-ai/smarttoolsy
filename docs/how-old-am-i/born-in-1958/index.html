How old am I if I was born in 1958?

Your age in 2026, plus the facts and milestones for the 1958 birth year.

68 years old

If you were born in 1958, you are 68 if you have already had your birthday in 2026, or 67 if your birthday is still to come this year.

How the answer is worked out

Subtract your birth year from the current year: 2026 − 1958 = 68. That is your age once your birthday has passed this year. Until then you are one year younger, 67. For example, someone born in March 1958 is 68 from March onward, while someone born in November 1958 stays 67 until their November birthday.

What generation is 1958?

People born in 1958 belong to the Baby Boomers. The Pew Research Center, whose definitions are the most widely used in the US, places this generation from 1946 to 1964. Generation boundaries are conventions rather than strict rules, so you may also see slightly different ranges elsewhere.

Chinese zodiac for 1958

1958 is the Year of the Dog. The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, and Lunar New Year falls in late January or February. If you were born in January or early February 1958, before that year’s Lunar New Year, your sign is the Rooster instead.

Retirement and Medicare

For people born in 1958, the Social Security Administration sets the full retirement age at 66 and 8 months. You can start reduced benefits as early as 62, which for this birth year was or will be 2020, and Medicare eligibility begins at 65, in 2023. The SSA notes that anyone born on January 1 should use the rules for the previous year.
