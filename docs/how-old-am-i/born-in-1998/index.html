How old am I if I was born in 1998?

Your age in 2026, plus the facts and milestones for the 1998 birth year.

28 years old

If you were born in 1998, you are 28 if you have already had your birthday in 2026, or 27 if your birthday is still to come this year.

How the answer is worked out

Subtract your birth year from the current year: 2026 − 1998 = 28. That is your age once your birthday has passed this year. Until then you are one year younger, 27. For example, someone born in March 1998 is 28 from March onward, while someone born in November 1998 stays 27 until their November birthday.

What generation is 1998?

People born in 1998 belong to Generation Z. The Pew Research Center, whose definitions are the most widely used in the US, places this generation from 1997 to 2012. Generation boundaries are conventions rather than strict rules, so you may also see slightly different ranges elsewhere.

Chinese zodiac for 1998

1998 is the Year of the Tiger. The Chinese zodiac follows the lunar calendar, and Lunar New Year falls in late January or February. If you were born in January or early February 1998, before that year’s Lunar New Year, your sign is the Ox instead.

Retirement and Medicare

For people born in 1998, the Social Security Administration sets the full retirement age at 67. You can start reduced benefits as early as 62, which for this birth year was or will be 2060, and Medicare eligibility begins at 65, in 2063. The SSA notes that anyone born on January 1 should use the rules for the previous year.
