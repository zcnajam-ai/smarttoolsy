What year was I born if I’m 63?

Your birth year from your age in 2026, with the facts that go with it.

Born in 1963 or 1962

If you are 63 years old in 2026, you were born in 1963 if you have already had your birthday this year, or in 1962 if your birthday is still to come.

How to work it out

Subtract your age from the current year: 2026 − 63 = 1963. That is your birth year if your birthday has already happened this year. If it hasn’t, subtract one more year: 1962. The same rule works for any age, and you can check the result the other way round with our born in 1963 page.

Milestones

Milestones you have already reached:

  • 16 (1978 or 1979): Full driver’s license possible in many US states
  • 18 (1980 or 1981): Voting age in US elections
  • 21 (1983 or 1984): Legal drinking age in the US
  • 25 (1987 or 1988): Car rental usually without a young-driver fee
  • 26 (1988 or 1989): Coverage on a parent’s health plan ends (Affordable Care Act)
  • 35 (1997 or 1998): Old enough to be President of the United States
  • 62 (2024 or 2025): Earliest age to claim (reduced) Social Security benefits

Milestones still ahead:

  • 65 (2027 or 2028): Medicare eligibility

If you were born in 1963, your Social Security full retirement age is 67; for 1962 it is 67.
