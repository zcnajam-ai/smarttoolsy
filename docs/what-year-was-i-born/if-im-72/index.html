What year was I born if I’m 72?

Your birth year from your age in 2026, with the facts that go with it.

Born in 1954 or 1953

If you are 72 years old in 2026, you were born in 1954 if you have already had your birthday this year, or in 1953 if your birthday is still to come.

How to work it out

Subtract your age from the current year: 2026 − 72 = 1954. That is your birth year if your birthday has already happened this year. If it hasn’t, subtract one more year: 1953. The same rule works for any age, and you can check the result the other way round with our born in 1954 page.

Milestones

Milestones you have already reached:

  • 16 (1969 or 1970): Full driver’s license possible in many US states
  • 18 (1971 or 1972): Voting age in US elections
  • 21 (1974 or 1975): Legal drinking age in the US
  • 25 (1978 or 1979): Car rental usually without a young-driver fee
  • 26 (1979 or 1980): Coverage on a parent’s health plan ends (Affordable Care Act)
  • 35 (1988 or 1989): Old enough to be President of the United States
  • 62 (2015 or 2016): Earliest age to claim (reduced) Social Security benefits
  • 65 (2018 or 2019): Medicare eligibility

If you were born in 1954, your Social Security full retirement age is 66; for 1953 it is 66.
