If I’m 6, what year was I born?

Your birth year from your age in 2026, with the facts that go with it.

Born in 2020 or 2019

If you are 6 years old in 2026, you were born in 2020 if you have already had your birthday this year, or in 2019 if your birthday is still to come.

How to work it out

Subtract your age from the current year: 2026 − 6 = 2020. That is your birth year if your birthday has already happened this year. If it hasn’t, subtract one more year: 2019. The same rule works for any age, and you can check the result the other way round with our born in 2020 page.

Frequently asked questions

If I’m 6, what year will I turn 16?

In 2036 if your birthday has passed this year, otherwise in 2035.

What year did I turn 18 if I’m 6?

You have not turned 18 yet. You will turn 18 in 2038 or 2037.
