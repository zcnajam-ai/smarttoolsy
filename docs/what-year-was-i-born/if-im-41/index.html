What year was I born if I’m 41?

Your birth year from your age in 2026, with the facts that go with it.

Born in 1985 or 1984

If you are 41 years old in 2026, you were born in 1985 if you have already had your birthday this year, or in 1984 if your birthday is still to come.

How to work it out

Subtract your age from the current year: 2026 − 41 = 1985. That is your birth year if your birthday has already happened this year. If it hasn’t, subtract one more year: 1984. The same rule works for any age, and you can check the result the other way round with our born in 1985 page.

Milestones

Milestones you have already reached:

  • 16 (2000 or 2001): Full driver’s license possible in many US states
  • 18 (2002 or 2003): Voting age in US elections
  • 21 (2005 or 2006): Legal drinking age in the US
  • 25 (2009 or 2010): Car rental usually without a young-driver fee
  • 26 (2010 or 2011): Coverage on a parent’s health plan ends (Affordable Care Act)
  • 35 (2019 or 2020): Old enough to be President of the United States

Milestones still ahead:

  • 62 (2046 or 2047): Earliest age to claim (reduced) Social Security benefits
  • 65 (2049 or 2050): Medicare eligibility

If you were born in 1985, your Social Security full retirement age is 67; for 1984 it is 67.
