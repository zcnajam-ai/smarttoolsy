What year was I born if I’m 82?

Your birth year from your age in 2026, with the facts that go with it.

Born in 1944 or 1943

If you are 82 years old in 2026, you were born in 1944 if you have already had your birthday this year, or in 1943 if your birthday is still to come.

How to work it out

Subtract your age from the current year: 2026 − 82 = 1944. That is your birth year if your birthday has already happened this year. If it hasn’t, subtract one more year: 1943. The same rule works for any age, and you can check the result the other way round with our born in 1944 page.

Milestones

Milestones you have already reached:

  • 16 (1959 or 1960): Full driver’s license possible in many US states
  • 18 (1961 or 1962): Voting age in US elections
  • 21 (1964 or 1965): Legal drinking age in the US
  • 25 (1968 or 1969): Car rental usually without a young-driver fee
  • 26 (1969 or 1970): Coverage on a parent’s health plan ends (Affordable Care Act)
  • 35 (1978 or 1979): Old enough to be President of the United States
  • 62 (2005 or 2006): Earliest age to claim (reduced) Social Security benefits
  • 65 (2008 or 2009): Medicare eligibility

If you were born in 1944, your Social Security full retirement age is 66; for 1943 it is 66.
