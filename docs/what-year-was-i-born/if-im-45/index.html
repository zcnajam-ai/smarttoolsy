What year was I born if I’m 45?

Your birth year from your age in 2026, with the facts that go with it.

Born in 1981 or 1980

If you are 45 years old in 2026, you were born in 1981 if you have already had your birthday this year, or in 1980 if your birthday is still to come.

How to work it out

Subtract your age from the current year: 2026 − 45 = 1981. That is your birth year if your birthday has already happened this year. If it hasn’t, subtract one more year: 1980. The same rule works for any age, and you can check the result the other way round with our born in 1981 page.

Milestones

Milestones you have already reached:

  • 16 (1996 or 1997): Full driver’s license possible in many US states
  • 18 (1998 or 1999): Voting age in US elections
  • 21 (2001 or 2002): Legal drinking age in the US
  • 25 (2005 or 2006): Car rental usually without a young-driver fee
  • 26 (2006 or 2007): Coverage on a parent’s health plan ends (Affordable Care Act)
  • 35 (2015 or 2016): Old enough to be President of the United States

Milestones still ahead:

  • 62 (2042 or 2043): Earliest age to claim (reduced) Social Security benefits
  • 65 (2045 or 2046): Medicare eligibility

If you were born in 1981, your Social Security full retirement age is 67; for 1980 it is 67.
