If I’m 22, what year was I born?

Your birth year from your age in 2026, with the facts that go with it.

Born in 2004 or 2003

If you are 22 years old in 2026, you were born in 2004 if you have already had your birthday this year, or in 2003 if your birthday is still to come.

How to work it out

Subtract your age from the current year: 2026 − 22 = 2004. That is your birth year if your birthday has already happened this year. If it hasn’t, subtract one more year: 2003. The same rule works for any age, and you can check the result the other way round with our born in 2004 page.

Milestones

Milestones you have already reached:

  • 16 (2019 or 2020): Full driver’s license possible in many US states
  • 18 (2021 or 2022): Voting age in US elections
  • 21 (2024 or 2025): Legal drinking age in the US

Milestones still ahead:

  • 25 (2028 or 2029): Car rental usually without a young-driver fee
  • 26 (2029 or 2030): Coverage on a parent’s health plan ends (Affordable Care Act)
  • 35 (2038 or 2039): Old enough to be President of the United States
  • 62 (2065 or 2066): Earliest age to claim (reduced) Social Security benefits
  • 65 (2068 or 2069): Medicare eligibility

If you were born in 2004, your Social Security full retirement age is 67; for 2003 it is 67.
