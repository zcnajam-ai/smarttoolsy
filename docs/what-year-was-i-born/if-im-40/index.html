What year was I born if I’m 40?

Your birth year from your age in 2026, with the facts that go with it.

Born in 1986 or 1985

If you are 40 years old in 2026, you were born in 1986 if you have already had your birthday this year, or in 1985 if your birthday is still to come.

How to work it out

Subtract your age from the current year: 2026 − 40 = 1986. That is your birth year if your birthday has already happened this year. If it hasn’t, subtract one more year: 1985. The same rule works for any age, and you can check the result the other way round with our born in 1986 page.

Milestones

Milestones you have already reached:

  • 16 (2001 or 2002): Full driver’s license possible in many US states
  • 18 (2003 or 2004): Voting age in US elections
  • 21 (2006 or 2007): Legal drinking age in the US
  • 25 (2010 or 2011): Car rental usually without a young-driver fee
  • 26 (2011 or 2012): Coverage on a parent’s health plan ends (Affordable Care Act)
  • 35 (2020 or 2021): Old enough to be President of the United States

Milestones still ahead:

  • 62 (2047 or 2048): Earliest age to claim (reduced) Social Security benefits
  • 65 (2050 or 2051): Medicare eligibility

If you were born in 1986, your Social Security full retirement age is 67; for 1985 it is 67.
