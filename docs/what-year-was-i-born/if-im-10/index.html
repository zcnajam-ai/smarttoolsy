What year was I born if I’m 10?

Your birth year from your age in 2026, with the facts that go with it.

Born in 2016 or 2015

If you are 10 years old in 2026, you were born in 2016 if you have already had your birthday this year, or in 2015 if your birthday is still to come.

How to work it out

Subtract your age from the current year: 2026 − 10 = 2016. That is your birth year if your birthday has already happened this year. If it hasn’t, subtract one more year: 2015. The same rule works for any age, and you can check the result the other way round with our born in 2016 page.

Frequently asked questions

If I’m 10, what year will I turn 20?

In 2036 if your birthday has passed this year, otherwise in 2035.

What year did I turn 18 if I’m 10?

You have not turned 18 yet. You will turn 18 in 2034 or 2033.
