How this is calculated

The year is simply this year minus the number of years. The full date shows the same month and day in that year; for February 29 in a non-leap year, February 28 is used.

For example, counted from Sunday, October 25, 2026, 60 years ago was Tuesday, October 25, 1966, 21915 days earlier.
