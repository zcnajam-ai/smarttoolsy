How the countdown works

The countdown uses the clock on your device and updates every second. If 6:15 AM has already passed today, it counts down to 6:15 AM tomorrow. 6:15 AM is 06:15 in 24-hour (military) time.

Handy for timing the end of a work shift, a class, a meeting, a cooking timer or a store opening. Keep the page open and the timer keeps running.
