How the countdown works

The countdown uses the clock on your device and updates every second. If 8:20 AM has already passed today, it counts down to 8:20 AM tomorrow. 8:20 AM is 08:20 in 24-hour (military) time.

Handy for timing the end of a work shift, a class, a meeting, a cooking timer or a store opening. Keep the page open and the timer keeps running.
